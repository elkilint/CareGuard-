Operating philosophy
Do more with less — automate everything that doesn't require a human
With 1–3 founders and under $50K, every hour of human time is precious. CareGuard's operational model is built on radical automation of repetitive tasks (onboarding, billing, support tier-1, AI monitoring) so the founding team can focus on the two things that only humans can do well at this stage: talking to customers and making product decisions. The org chart expands only when a specific function is provably overwhelmed.

Hardware supply chain
Lean, outsourced, asset-light
Step 1
Sourcing
Off-the-shelf Zigbee/Z-Wave sensors (Aqara / Sonoff). Custom firmware + CareGuard branding. Min order: 200 units.
Lead: 8–12 weeks
Step 2
Assembly & QA
3PL partner does kitting + quality check + packaging. CareGuard-branded box with quick-start guide (EN + ES).
Lead: 1–2 weeks
Step 3
Fulfillment
ShipBob (US) / Melonn (LATAM). Orders triggered automatically at checkout. Real-time tracking in app.
US: 3–5 days
Step 4
Returns
30-day return window. Defective unit replacements shipped same day. Returned units refurbished and resold at 10% discount.
Target: <3% return rate
Step 5
Inventory mgmt
6-week safety stock target. Reorder triggered at 4-week coverage. Demand forecast from subscriber growth model.
Review: monthly
Technology stack
Year 1 stack — lean, proven tools, minimal build
Mobile app
React Native
Single codebase for iOS + Android. Expo for rapid iteration. Push notifications via Firebase. Family dashboard + alert feed.
~$0/mo (dev cost only)
IoT & sensors
AWS IoT Core
MQTT protocol for sensor data. DynamoDB for time-series storage. Lambda for real-time event processing. Custom anomaly detection model.
~$200–400/mo @ 1K subs
AI / ML
Python + SageMaker
Behavioral baseline model per subscriber. Anomaly scoring engine. Retrained weekly per user. Initially rule-based, evolves to full ML by Year 2.
~$300–600/mo @ 1K subs
Backend / API
Node.js + Supabase
REST API for app. Supabase for auth + postgres. Stripe for billing. Serverless functions for alert routing and notifications.
~$150/mo @ 1K subs
Comms & alerts
Twilio + SendGrid
SMS alerts (Twilio). Email sequences (SendGrid). WhatsApp Business API for LATAM users. Push via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
~$200/mo @ 1K subs
CRM & Support
Intercom + Stripe
In-app chat, onboarding flows, NPS surveys (Intercom). Subscription billing + dunning (Stripe). Referral tracking via ReferralHero.
~$300/mo @ 1K subs
Total estimated infra cost at 1,000 subscribers: ~$1,150–1,650/mo (~9% of sub revenue — well within the 18% COGS target).

Risk register
Top risks and mitigation strategies
Risk Severity Likelihood Impact Mitigation
Hardware supply delay — manufacturer lead times extend High Medium Can't ship kits → trials stall → cash burns on marketing with no conversions Maintain 6-week safety stock. Dual-source from 2 suppliers. Offer "pre-order with discount" to manage demand.
AI false alert rate too high — erodes family trust High Medium High churn, negative NPS, word-of-mouth reversal — the worst outcome for a safety product 7-day learning period before alerts go live. Manual override always available. Feedback loop retrains model continuously. Alert rate KPI reviewed weekly.
High early churn — trial users don't convert High Medium CAC payback never achieved, burn accelerates, model breaks down Founders personally onboard first 50 families. Exit survey on every cancel. Build "sticky moment" — ensure family receives one real alert in first 30 days.
LATAM payment infrastructure — local methods complex Medium High LATAM conversion rates suffer; diaspora family paying for parent abroad is easier path Phase 1: diaspora pays in USD for LATAM parent. Phase 2: integrate Mercado Pago + PSE via Stripe LATAM. Don't over-build locally before proving demand.
Competitor enters the space — Ring, Google add elder care mode Medium Low Could commoditize the product in the US market within 18–24 months Own the LATAM + diaspora niche — big tech won't localize for this. Build proprietary behavioral AI as the defensible moat. Speed of iteration beats resources.
Founder burnout — 1–3 people doing everything Medium Medium Product quality degrades, customer response slows, co-founder conflicts emerge Automate Tier 1 support from Day 1. Hard rule: no founder works more than 55hrs/week sustained. First hire at 200 subs covers CS load. Equity vesting with cliff protects against early split.
Data privacy / regulatory — health data laws in US + LATAM Medium Low HIPAA, GDPR-LATAM, CCPA exposure if not handled correctly from Day 1 Legal review of privacy policy before launch ($2–4K). No biometric data collected — motion only. Data stored in country of origin where possible. No data sold, ever.
