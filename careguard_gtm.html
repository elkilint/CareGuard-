Go-to-Market Strategy

B2C · Under $50K budget · Digital + Referral · LATAM, US & Canada · 2026–2028
GTM Philosophy
Start tight, go deep, then expand
With a sub-$50K budget and a 1–3 person team, the worst mistake is trying to be everywhere at once. CareGuard's GTM is built on one principle: own a specific beachhead segment so completely that word-of-mouth does the heavy lifting by month 6. That segment is Latin American diaspora families in the US — adults aged 30–50, living in major US metros, with parents or grandparents in Colombia, Mexico, or Chile. They have the income, the urgency, and the emotional motivation. And they talk to each other.

Beachhead segment
Who we go after first — and why
Primary: US-based Latino diaspora
Age 32–52. Household income $60K+. Parent or grandparent in LATAM (Colombia, Mexico, Chile). Uses WhatsApp daily. Follows Latino health/family influencers. Strong community ties — refers to siblings and cousins. Pain: distance guilt + no reliable remote care option.
Secondary: Local LATAM families
Middle-class families in Bogotá, Medellín, CDMX, Santiago. Adult children working long hours, parents living alone or with conditions. Price-sensitive — LATAM pricing required. Acquired primarily through referral from diaspora segment.
Tertiary: Canadian diaspora (Phase 3)
Similar profile to US segment. Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary. Latin American immigrant communities with family abroad. Add in Year 2 once playbook is proven in the US.
Acquisition channels
Budget allocation — Year 1 ($50K total)

40% · $20K
Target Hispanic/Latino adults 30–55 in Miami, Houston, NYC, LA. Interest targeting: "family caregiver," "parents abroad," "home safety." Spanish-language creatives. Video ads showing the emotional moment — a child relieved after getting a safety update on their parent. Test 5+ creatives, kill fast.

20% · $10K
Give-$15/get-$15 credit for both referrer and new subscriber. Activated at day 14 (post-trial, high satisfaction window). WhatsApp-native sharing — pre-written message in Spanish. Family referral bonus: if a sibling signs up, both get 2 free months. Budget covers incentive credits and tooling.

Content & SEO
15% · $7.5K
Spanish + English blog targeting high-intent searches: "how to monitor elderly parent remotely," "cómo cuidar a mis padres desde lejos." YouTube shorts: real stories, product demos. Repurposed as ad creative. 3–6 month play — builds compounding organic traffic.
Target: 5K monthly organic visitors by month 12
Community & WhatsApp
15% · $7.5K
Partner with Latino family Facebook groups, WhatsApp community admins, and Latino parenting communities. Sponsored posts + moderator outreach. Attend 2–3 Latino community events in target cities (e.g., health fairs, senior fairs). Budget covers events + community sponsorships.
Target: 200 leads/month by month 9
Micro-influencers
10% · $5K
5–10 Latino family/health micro-influencers (10K–100K followers). Free product + $300–600 per post. Focus on authentic testimonial format — "I set this up for my mom in Bogotá." Instagram reels, TikTok. Measure by tracked referral links, not vanity metrics.
Target: 50+ conversions per campaign

Referral engine
The growth flywheel — designed for WhatsApp culture
Give $15 · Get $15 · Family bonus: 2 free months
The referral program is CareGuard's most important long-term acquisition channel. Latino families communicate via WhatsApp — and when something genuinely helps a parent stay safe, they share it. The program is engineered for that behavior.
1
Subscriber completes 14-day trial → receives referral link + pre-written WhatsApp message in Spanish
2
Friend clicks link → sees landing page with referrer's first name ("Maria thinks you need this") → starts free trial
3
Friend converts to paid → both receive $15 credit. If friend is a sibling → family bonus triggers (2 free months for both)
4
New subscriber enters the same referral loop at day 14 → flywheel compounds

12-month milestones
Quarterly targets and key activities
Q1
Foundation — build the machine
Launch landing page (EN + ES). Set up Meta Ads account and first 3 creatives. Manually onboard first 30 families in Miami. Build referral tooling. Set up analytics (Mixpanel or Amplitude). Begin WhatsApp community outreach.
Target: 50 active subscribers · CAC < $60 (learning phase)
Q2
Prove — validate the flywheel
Activate referral program. Expand Meta Ads to Houston + NYC. Launch first micro-influencer campaign (3 creators). Publish 10 SEO blog posts. First LATAM soft launch (Colombia — friends and family).
Target: 200 active subscribers · NPS > 55 · first referral conversions
Q3
Scale — pour fuel on what works
Double budget on best-performing Meta creative. Expand to LA + Chicago. Launch Colombia and Mexico officially. WhatsApp group partnerships. Begin building caregiver/pharmacy partnership pipeline.
Target: 600 active subscribers · CAC < $40 · MRR > $11K
Q4
Momentum — data-driven retention focus
Launch annual plan discount (2 months free). First retention campaign targeting month-3 risk cohort. Publish case studies. Begin Canadian market research. Prepare seed deck with unit economics proof.
Target: 1,000 active subscribers · MRR > $19K · churn < 3%/mo
