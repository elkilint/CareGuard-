+ · Critical FAQ
What if they don't want to be "monitored"?
The design starts from consent. No cameras, no mics. The elderly person can pause alerts anytime with a physical button.
How many false alarms will I receive?
Our AI filters noise. Once calibrated, users typically receive only 2-3 meaningful alerts per week.
