4 · The problem
Millions of families live with the constant anxiety of not knowing what's happening when they're not there.
An elderly parent living alone, a father with diabetes, a grandmother with early-onset dementia — these situations create relentless worry for their families. The current options are extreme: hire a full-time caregiver (expensive and intrusive), install cameras (which violate privacy and are often rejected), or simply wait for the phone to ring. The result is a family living with chronic anxiety and an elderly person who feels their dignity slipping away. Every year, thousands of falls, medical episodes, and emergencies go undetected for too long — because no one was watching.
5 · The solution
Intelligent presence, not surveillance.
CareGuard installs motion, temperature, and door sensors at the key points of the home. The AI learns the elderly person's daily patterns — what time they wake up, when they have breakfast, how long they spend in the bathroom. When something deviates from normal, the family gets a clear alert on their phone. No cameras. No microphones. No constant notifications. Just a signal when something needs attention. The family dashboard lets multiple people — children in different cities, the primary care physician, a visiting nurse — share the same real-time information and coordinate their response.

7 · How it works
From unboxing to peace of mind in under an hour.
  1. 1
    The family downloads the app and creates a profile for their loved one, entering daily routines, health conditions, and emergency contacts.
  2. 2
    The sensor kit arrives at the home. Installation requires no tools — sensors simply stick on doors, hallways, and the kitchen, and connect to the home's WiFi from within the app.
  3. 3
    During the first 7 days, the AI learns the person's lifestyle rhythm. The family can see a daily summary ("Mom got up at 7am, had breakfast, went to the garden").
  4. 4
    If something is off — no movement for 2 hours during active hours, the fridge door hasn't opened all day — the family gets an alert with a priority level and a suggested action.
  5. 5
    In the event of a confirmed emergency, CareGuard can directly contact the local emergency service with the person's location and basic medical history.
  6. 6
    The family dashboard allows access to be shared with other caregivers, the doctor, or a home care agency, with differentiated permission levels for each.

+ · Critical FAQ
What if the elderly person doesn't want to be "monitored"?
The design starts from their consent. There are no cameras or microphones. The sensors detect presence and movement, not identity. The elderly person has a physical button to pause all alerts during private moments. The experience is framed as "safety," not surveillance.
How many false alarms will I receive?
Our AI learns individual patterns. After the first 7 days, 90% of notifications reflect genuine anomalies. The goal is for families to receive no more than 2–3 alerts per week — not dozens of notifications that become noise.
What happens if the power or WiFi goes out?
The sensors have a 48-hour backup battery. If they lose connection for more than 30 minutes, the family receives a "signal lost" alert so they can check in. The system also runs in local mode during power outages.
Can multiple family members have access?
Yes. A family plan includes unlimited access for family members plus a professional access for the doctor or hired caregiver, with differentiated permissions. Everyone sees the same real-time status.
