4 · The problem
Millions of families live with the constant anxiety of not knowing.

An elderly parent living alone creates relentless worry. The current options are extreme: expensive caregivers, intrusive cameras, or simply waiting for the phone to ring. The result is a family living with chronic anxiety.

5 · The solution
Intelligent presence, not surveillance.

CareGuard installs motion, temperature, and door sensors. The AI learns the elderly person's daily patterns. When something deviates from normal, the family gets a clear alert on their phone. No cameras. No microphones. Just a signal when something needs attention.

7 · How it works
From unboxing to peace of mind in under an hour.
  1. 1
    Profile Setup: The family downloads the app and creates a profile with routines and emergency contacts.
  2. 2
    Easy Install: Wireless sensors simply stick on doors and hallways (no tools needed).
  3. 3
    AI Learning: During the first 7 days, the AI learns the person's lifestyle rhythm.
  4. 4
    Smart Alerts: If something is off — no movement for 2 hours during active hours — the family gets an alert.
  5. 5
    Emergency Protocol: CareGuard can contact local emergency services with location and medical history.
  6. 6
    Shared Care: Access can be shared with siblings, doctors, or nurses with differentiated permissions.

+ · Critical FAQ
What if the elderly person doesn't want to be "monitored"?
No cameras or microphones. The experience is framed as safety, and there is a physical button to pause alerts during private moments.
How many false alarms will I receive?
The AI filters noise. After calibration, families typically receive only 2–3 meaningful alerts per week.
What if the power or WiFi goes out?
Sensors have a 48-hour backup battery. If connection is lost for 30 minutes, the family receives a "signal lost" alert.
Can multiple family members have access?
Yes. A family plan includes unlimited access for family members plus professional access for caregivers or doctors.
